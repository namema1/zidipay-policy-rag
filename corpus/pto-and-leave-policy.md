# PTO and Leave Policy

**Document ID:** ZDP-HR-002
**Effective date:** 2024-02-01
**Owner:** People Operations
**Applies to:** All Zidipay permanent and fixed-term employees in Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, and Rwanda

## 1. Annual leave

Permanent employees accrue **24 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued at **2 days per month** worked. New joiners may take leave once they have completed three months of service; before that, leave is accrued but not yet eligible to be taken except in exceptional circumstances approved by People Operations.

Fixed-term employees accrue annual leave at the same monthly rate, pro-rated to their contract length. Interns accrue **1 day per month**, statutory minimum.

## 2. How to request annual leave

1. Submit the leave request in **Zidipay HRMS** at least **10 working days** before the first day of leave for any request longer than 3 days, and at least 2 working days in advance for shorter requests.
2. Your line manager approves or declines within 3 working days.
3. Once approved, leave is reflected in your shared calendar and your team handover document is required for absences of 5 or more days.

## 3. Carry-over

Up to **5 days** of unused annual leave may be carried into the next calendar year and must be used by **31 March**. Carried-over days that are unused by that date are forfeited. Carry-over beyond 5 days requires written approval from the Head of People and is only granted where business reasons prevented the employee from taking the leave.

## 4. Sick leave

Employees are entitled to **14 days** of paid sick leave per calendar year, separate from annual leave. After 14 days of paid sick leave in a rolling 12-month period, additional sick leave is paid at 50% for up to a further 14 days, after which it is unpaid pending review by People Operations.

A medical certificate from a licensed practitioner is required for any sick leave of **3 consecutive days or more**, and for any sick leave on a day immediately before or after annual leave or a public holiday.

## 5. Parental leave

- **Maternity leave:** 90 calendar days fully paid, starting any time from 4 weeks before the expected due date. An additional 30 days unpaid leave is available on request.
- **Paternity leave:** 21 calendar days fully paid, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.
- **Adoption leave:** Same as maternity or paternity depending on the primary caregiver designation.
- **Pregnancy loss leave:** 7 calendar days fully paid, separate from sick leave.

Notice of intent to take parental leave should be given at least 30 days in advance where reasonably possible.

## 6. Compassionate leave

**5 working days** of fully paid compassionate leave per event, available for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, parent-in-law). Additional unpaid leave may be granted by your manager.

## 7. Study leave

Employees enrolled in a course approved under the learning budget may take up to **5 working days** of paid study leave per calendar year for exams or final project work, with proof of registration.

## 8. Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave of up to 30 calendar days may be granted at the manager's and Head of People's joint discretion once annual leave has been exhausted. Benefits (health insurance, pension contributions) continue during unpaid leave funded by the employee unless otherwise agreed in writing.

## 9. Public holidays

Public holidays are paid days off — see `Public Holidays and Working Hours` for the full list per country.

## 10. Leave during notice period

Employees serving notice may take accrued, unused annual leave subject to manager approval. Unused accrued leave at the end of employment is paid out at base salary at the rate of (annual base salary ÷ 260) per day.

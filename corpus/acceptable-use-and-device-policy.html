Acceptable Use and Device Policy

Document ID: ZDP-SEC-003
Effective date: 2024-01-15
Owner: Chief Information Security Officer
Applies to: All employees, contractors, and interns using Zidipay devices or accessing Zidipay systems

1. Purpose

This policy defines acceptable use of Zidipay-issued devices, the very narrow circumstances under which personal devices ("BYOD") may be used, and the activities that are prohibited on Zidipay systems. It complements the Information Security Policy.

2. Issued devices

Every full-time employee is issued a company laptop, configured by IT before handover. The default configuration includes:

  • Full-disk encryption (FileVault on macOS, BitLocker on Windows) — enabled and verified.
  • Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR) agent — must not be disabled.
  • Mobile Device Management (MDM) — must not be removed.
  • VPN client (Tailscale) — pre-installed.
  • Approved software (browser, IDE for engineers, productivity apps).

Employees may install additional software only from the IT-managed approved-software list. Requests to add software to the approved list go through the IT Service Desk and are reviewed by Security within 5 working days.

3. Personal use of company devices

Reasonable personal use of company devices is permitted, provided it:

  • does not interfere with Zidipay work,
  • does not consume excessive bandwidth or storage,
  • does not introduce malware or risk Restricted data, and
  • is consistent with this policy and the Code of Conduct.

Employees should have no expectation of personal privacy on company devices: Zidipay may monitor, inspect, and audit company devices at any time, subject to local employment law.

4. BYOD (Bring Your Own Device)

Personal devices may be used only for the following narrow purposes, and only after enrolling in the Zidipay MDM-lite profile:

  • Reading Zidipay email and Slack from a mobile phone.
  • Multi-factor authentication apps where the YubiKey is unavailable temporarily.

Personal laptops are not permitted for accessing Zidipay production systems, customer data, source code, or any Restricted or Confidential data. Engineers who must work from a personal laptop in an emergency must escalate to the IT Service Desk first.

5. Prohibited use

The following activities are prohibited on Zidipay devices, networks, and accounts:

  1. Sharing Zidipay credentials with anyone, including colleagues. Use IT-managed group accounts where shared access is needed.
  2. Disabling or tampering with the EDR agent, MDM, disk encryption, or VPN client.
  3. Installing pirated, cracked, or unlicensed software.
  4. Accessing illegal, sexually explicit, hateful, or extremist material.
  5. Using Zidipay devices, accounts, or data for personal commercial gain or another employer's work.
  6. Connecting Zidipay devices to public charging ports (USB charging in airports and cafés).
  7. Connecting Zidipay devices to home or office printers that are not approved for confidential printing.
  8. Copying Restricted data to personal storage (cloud or USB).
  9. Using personal email to send Zidipay information of any classification above Public.

6. Lost, stolen, or damaged devices

Report loss or theft immediately by calling +254 711 000 999 and emailing security@zidipay.example. Do not attempt to track the device yourself. IT will remotely wipe the device. Damaged devices should be reported to the IT Service Desk; the first incident per employee per year is repaired or replaced at no cost.

7. End of employment

All Zidipay devices must be returned on or before the last working day per the Onboarding and Offboarding Procedure. Failure to return company equipment may result in the device value being deducted from the final pay where local law permits.

8. Sanctions

Breaches of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including dismissal, and where appropriate, criminal referral.

# Expense and Reimbursement Policy

**Document ID:** ZDP-FIN-001
**Effective date:** 2024-02-15
**Owner:** Finance, Accounts Payable
**Applies to:** All Zidipay employees claiming reimbursement of business expenses

## 1. Principles

Business expenses must be (a) reasonable, (b) directly necessary for Zidipay work, (c) supported by an itemised receipt, and (d) submitted within 30 days of being incurred. Personal expenses, alcohol consumed outside an approved client event, and fines (including parking and traffic fines) are never reimbursable.

## 2. Per diems while travelling on company business

| Location | Per diem (KES equivalent) |
| --- | --- |
| Domestic (Kenya, your home country office) | KES 6,500 per night |
| Within East Africa (UG, TZ, RW from your home country) | KES 9,500 per night |
| Outside East Africa | USD 80 per night |

The per diem covers meals and incidentals when meals are not otherwise provided. Where a meal is provided (for example, by a hotel or a client), reduce the per diem by KES 1,500 per provided meal.

## 3. Meal and client-entertainment limits

| Category | Daily limit (per person) |
| --- | --- |
| Solo meal while travelling (no per diem claimed) | KES 3,500 lunch / KES 5,500 dinner |
| Internal team meal | KES 4,000 |
| **Client dinner** | **KES 8,000 per head** |
| Off-site / quarterly team event | Pre-approved budget only |

Alcohol may only be claimed as part of a pre-approved client dinner or company event, capped at one drink per person.

## 4. Travel: cabs and ground transport

- Airport transfers: actual cost of an official taxi or company-approved e-hail service (Bolt, Uber).
- Local commuting to office is not reimbursable.
- Late-night commute home from the office after 21:00 local time is reimbursable up to KES 1,500 per trip.

## 5. Hotels and accommodation

Book through the company travel agent (Maridadi Travel) where possible. Self-booked accommodation is reimbursable up to:

- Nairobi / Dar es Salaam / Kampala / Kigali: KES 12,000 per night.
- Other African cities: KES 15,000 per night.
- Outside Africa: USD 200 per night.

See `Travel Policy` for booking process, travel-class rules, and travel advances.

## 6. Connectivity and home-office allowance

Hybrid and remote employees may claim **KES 4,000 per month** as a connectivity allowance (no receipt required). This replaces ad-hoc home internet claims. The one-time home-office stipend is described in the `Remote and Hybrid Work Policy`.

## 7. Approval thresholds

| Total expense (single submission) | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Up to KES 50,000 | Line manager |
| KES 50,001 – KES 250,000 | Head of Department |
| KES 250,001 – KES 1,000,000 | CFO |
| Above KES 1,000,000 | CFO and CEO |

Expenses must not be split across submissions to avoid a higher approval threshold; doing so is grounds for disciplinary action.

## 8. Submission process

1. Submit via **Zidipay Expensify** within **30 days** of incurring the expense.
2. Attach an itemised receipt for every line; bank statements are not accepted.
3. Tag the project/cost-centre.
4. Your line manager (or higher per §7) approves.
5. Finance pays approved claims on the next monthly run (25th in Kenya, 28th elsewhere).

## 9. Foreign-currency expenses

Expenses incurred in a foreign currency are reimbursed at the mid-market exchange rate on the date of the expense (Finance uses oanda.com as the reference source).

## 10. Misuse

False or inflated claims, splitting claims to avoid approval thresholds, or repeated late submissions are disciplinary matters and may amount to gross misconduct. Suspected fraud will be escalated via the whistleblowing channel and to the Audit Committee.

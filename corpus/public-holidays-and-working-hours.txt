ZIDIPAY FINANCIAL TECHNOLOGIES
PUBLIC HOLIDAYS AND WORKING HOURS

Document ID: ZDP-HR-006
Effective date: 2024-01-01
Owner: People Operations

1. WORKING HOURS

Standard working hours are 08:00 to 17:00 local time, Monday through Friday,
with a one-hour lunch break taken between 12:00 and 14:00 at the employee's
discretion.

Core hours, when all employees must be reachable on Slack and Zoom, are
10:00 to 15:00 local time. Hours outside core may be flexed with manager
approval. Employees on shift work (Customer Support 24/7 rota, on-call SRE)
follow the schedule published in the shift planner.

2. OVERTIME

Overtime applies to employees in non-managerial roles at Level 3 and below
who work more than 45 hours in a week. Overtime is paid at:

  - 1.5x base hourly rate for hours over 45 in a weekday week,
  - 2.0x base hourly rate for hours worked on a rest day (typically Saturday),
  - 2.0x base hourly rate for hours worked on a public holiday.

Overtime must be authorised by the line manager in writing in advance.
Unauthorised overtime is not reimbursable.

7. HOLIDAYS FALLING ON A WEEKEND

Where a public holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, employees do not
automatically receive a day off in lieu unless the relevant national
government gazettes one. Country leads in People Operations will confirm
by email at the start of each year.

8. CHRISTMAS CLOSE-DOWN

Zidipay closes from 24 December (last working day before Christmas Eve) and
reopens on the first working day in January. Critical functions (Operations,
Customer Support, on-call SRE, Security) operate a reduced rota during the
close-down; staff on that rota receive a compensatory day off in January.

9. CALENDAR

A single shared "Zidipay Holidays" Google Calendar shows all four countries'
holidays. Employees should subscribe at onboarding. Country-specific holiday
calendars are also available on request.

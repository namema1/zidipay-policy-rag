# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

**Document ID:** ZDP-HR-003
**Effective date:** 2024-03-01
**Owner:** People Operations, with Security review

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines who may work remotely or hybrid at Zidipay, the expectations for those who do, and the security and equipment requirements that apply.

## 2. Work arrangements

Zidipay supports three arrangements:

- **In-office** — five days per week from your assigned office.
- **Hybrid (default for most roles)** — at least **3 days per week in the office**, of which **Tuesday and Wednesday are mandatory anchor days** company-wide. The third day is set by the team manager.
- **Fully remote** — available case-by-case for roles at Level 4 and above whose work does not require regular in-person collaboration, subject to Head of Department and Head of People approval.

## 3. Eligibility

Eligibility for hybrid or fully remote work requires:

- Completion of the **3-month probation period**.
- A current performance rating of "Meets expectations" or higher.
- A role that does not require physical presence (e.g., facilities, reception, on-site IT support are office-only).
- A home workspace that meets the security requirements in §6.

New joiners are office-based for their first three months unless an explicit written exception is granted at offer stage.

## 4. Hours and availability

Remote and hybrid employees keep the same working hours and core hours as in-office employees (see `Employee Handbook` §3). You must be reachable on Slack and Zoom during core hours (10:00 to 15:00 local time) regardless of location.

Working from a different time zone (more than 3 hours from your home office) for more than **10 consecutive working days** requires written approval.

## 5. Equipment

- Zidipay supplies a company laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse, and headset to every full-time remote or hybrid employee within 5 working days of their start date.
- A one-time **home-office stipend of KES 60,000** (or local equivalent) is paid in the first month to cover a chair, desk, and lighting. Receipts are not required, but the stipend is taxable.
- Internet and electricity are the employee's responsibility. Employees may claim **KES 4,000 per month** as a connectivity allowance via the `Expense and Reimbursement Policy`.

## 6. Security expectations for remote work

Remote work must comply with the `Information Security Policy` and the `Acceptable Use and Device Policy`. Specific home-office requirements:

- Only the company-issued laptop may be used to access Zidipay systems. Personal devices are not permitted except as described in the BYOD section of the device policy.
- Home Wi-Fi must use WPA2 or WPA3 with a unique password (not the router default).
- The laptop screen must not be visible to non-household members. When working in a public space (café, airport), use the issued privacy screen and never leave the device unattended.
- Sensitive customer data may not be printed at home.
- Report any suspected security incident immediately to security@zidipay.example or call +254 711 000 999.

## 7. International travel while working remotely

Working from outside your home country for **up to 30 calendar days per year** is permitted with manager approval. Periods longer than 30 days require Head of People and Tax approval because of payroll and permanent-establishment risk.

## 8. Returning to office-based work

The company may, with **30 days written notice**, require an employee to return to office-based work where business needs justify it (for example, a change in role, regulatory requirement, or sustained performance concern).

## 9. Termination of remote arrangement

Hybrid or remote arrangements may be revoked if the employee falls below "Meets expectations" performance for two consecutive cycles or breaches the security expectations in §6.

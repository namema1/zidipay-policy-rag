# Performance Review and Promotion Policy

**Document ID:** ZDP-HR-004
**Effective date:** 2024-02-01
**Owner:** People Operations
**Applies to:** All Zidipay permanent and fixed-term employees

## 1. Review cycles

Zidipay runs **two formal performance reviews per year**:

- **Mid-year review:** July, calibration in August.
- **End-of-year review:** January, calibration in February. Annual rating and any compensation adjustment are confirmed by 28 February.

In addition, every employee should expect **monthly one-to-ones** with their line manager and a **quarterly check-in** that updates goals.

## 2. Ratings

Performance is rated on a five-point scale:

| Rating | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Exceeds significantly** | Consistently delivers well beyond the expectations of the role. Top ~10% expected. |
| **Exceeds** | Delivers above the expectations of the role on most dimensions. |
| **Meets expectations** | Delivers on the expectations of the role. The healthy default. |
| **Partially meets** | Misses on one or more material dimensions. Triggers a development plan (§5). |
| **Does not meet** | Consistent failure to meet the expectations of the role. May lead to a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) per §5. |

Ratings are calibrated across teams to keep them comparable.

## 3. Promotion criteria

Promotion is **decoupled from the review cycle** — promotions can be proposed at either calibration cycle. To be considered for promotion to the next level, an employee must:

1. Have **operated at the next level for at least 6 months**, evidenced by concrete examples.
2. Hold a current rating of **"Exceeds"** or higher.
3. Receive endorsement from their line manager and one cross-functional sponsor.
4. Pass a panel review with at least three managers (one outside the employee's direct chain).

Time-in-grade alone is not a promotion criterion.

## 4. Compensation changes

Annual salary increases are tied to the end-of-year rating, country market data, and the company budget. Promotion increases are paid from the first of the month following the panel decision.

## 5. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

A PIP is a structured 60–90 day plan with specific, measurable success criteria. PIPs are confidential and supportive in intent: their purpose is to help an employee recover to "Meets expectations" or above.

- **Triggered by:** a "Partially meets" or "Does not meet" rating, or a sustained drop in performance flagged at any time of year.
- **Cadence:** weekly check-ins with the line manager; bi-weekly with HRBP.
- **Outcome:** if success criteria are met by the end of the plan, the employee returns to normal cycle; if not, the company may terminate employment in line with local employment law.

## 6. Appeals

An employee may appeal their rating within **14 calendar days** of receiving it. Appeals go to the Head of People, who appoints a reviewer outside the employee's direct chain.

## 7. Confidentiality and records

Performance records are Confidential data under the `Information Security Policy`. Access is restricted to the employee, their line manager, the relevant skip-level, People Operations, and (during calibration) the calibration panel.

# Travel Policy

**Document ID:** ZDP-FIN-002
**Effective date:** 2024-03-01
**Owner:** Finance, with People Operations input
**Applies to:** All Zidipay employees travelling on company business

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the rules for booking, classes of travel, advances, and safety while travelling on Zidipay business. The per-diem and meal limits while travelling are covered separately in the `Expense and Reimbursement Policy`.

## 2. Booking

All flights, hotels, and ground transport must be booked through the company travel agent **Maridadi Travel** (travel@maridadi.example, +254 700 555 222) using your assigned cost centre. Self-booking is only permitted where Maridadi cannot confirm a booking within 4 working hours and a written exception has been logged with Finance.

Bookings should be made at least:

- **14 calendar days** in advance for international travel.
- **7 calendar days** in advance for regional travel within East Africa.
- **3 working days** in advance for domestic travel.

Late bookings require written manager approval and the additional cost may be charged to the cost centre.

## 3. Class of travel

| Trip type | Flight | Train / Bus | Ground transport |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Domestic | Economy | Standard / first available | Official taxi or company-approved e-hail |
| Regional (East Africa) | Economy | Standard | Official taxi or e-hail |
| International, ≤ 6 hours | Economy | n/a | As above |
| International, > 6 hours | Premium economy where available, otherwise economy | n/a | As above |
| C-suite (CEO, CFO, COO, CTO) | Business class for flights > 4 hours | n/a | As above |

Upgrades using personal frequent-flyer miles or airline status are permitted at no cost to the company.

## 4. Travel advances

Travel advances of up to **KES 75,000** (or local equivalent) may be requested via Zidipay Expensify and must be:

1. Approved by the line manager.
2. Disbursed no earlier than 5 working days before travel.
3. Acquitted with receipts within **14 days of return**. Unspent advance must be returned in the same window.

Outstanding unacquitted advances older than 30 days will be deducted from the next salary run.

## 5. Visas, vaccinations, and travel insurance

- Visa fees and required vaccinations for business travel are fully reimbursable.
- Zidipay maintains a group business-travel insurance policy that covers all employees on company travel. The policy certificate is available from People Operations.

## 6. Safety and incidents

Before any international or non-home-country regional travel:

1. Submit your itinerary in the People Operations travel tracker.
2. Note the local emergency contacts provided in the pre-trip briefing email.
3. Carry the 24/7 Zidipay duty number: **+254 711 000 700**.

In the event of an incident (medical emergency, security incident, lost passport), call the duty number first, then follow up by email to people@zidipay.example.

## 7. Combining business and personal travel

Personal extensions of a business trip ("bleisure") are permitted with manager approval, provided:

- The personal portion does not increase the cost of the flight by more than 15%. Any excess is borne by the employee.
- The personal portion is taken as annual leave.
- Travel insurance during the personal portion is the employee's responsibility.

## 8. Spouse or family travel

Zidipay does not fund travel for spouses or family members on business trips.

## 9. Currency and reimbursement

See the `Expense and Reimbursement Policy` for currency conversion rules and submission deadlines.

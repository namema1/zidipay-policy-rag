# Zidipay Employee Handbook

**Document ID:** ZDP-HR-001
**Effective date:** 2024-01-15
**Owner:** People Operations
**Applies to:** All Zidipay employees, contractors, and interns across all offices

## 1. About Zidipay

Zidipay Financial Technologies Ltd. ("Zidipay") is an East-African digital payments and mobile wallet company founded in 2018 and headquartered in Nairobi, Kenya. We operate consumer wallets and merchant payment rails in Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, and Rwanda, with regional offices in Nairobi, Kampala, Dar es Salaam, and Kigali. Our mission is to make every payment in East Africa instant, affordable, and secure.

This handbook is the canonical summary of how we work at Zidipay. Topic-specific policies (PTO, security, expense, AML/KYC, etc.) extend this document and take precedence on the specific area they cover.

## 2. Our values

1. **Customer trust first.** We move money for millions of users — every decision is filtered through whether it earns or risks customer trust.
2. **Compliance is a feature.** As a regulated payments provider, compliance with the Central Bank of Kenya, Bank of Uganda, BoT, and BNR is non-negotiable.
3. **Bias to clarity.** Short documents, explicit owners, written decisions.
4. **Direct and kind.** We give feedback early, in person where possible, and assume good intent.

## 3. Working hours

Standard working hours are **08:00 to 17:00** local time, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. **Core hours** when all employees are expected to be reachable are **10:00 to 15:00** local time. Hours outside core may be flexed with manager approval. Detailed overtime, on-call, and public holiday treatment is covered in `Public Holidays and Working Hours`.

## 4. Employment classes

- **Permanent employees** — full benefits, including paid leave, health cover, and pension contributions.
- **Fixed-term employees** — full benefits pro-rated to contract length (minimum 6 months).
- **Interns** — paid stipend, statutory leave only, no pension.
- **Contractors** — paid per agreement; not eligible for company benefits.

## 5. Compensation

Salaries are reviewed annually as part of the performance cycle (see `Performance Review and Promotion Policy`). Compensation in Kenya is paid in KES on the 25th of each month; Uganda, Tanzania, and Rwanda payroll is paid on the 28th in local currency. Bonuses, where awarded, are paid in March following the prior calendar year.

## 6. Benefits summary

- **Health insurance:** Comprehensive inpatient and outpatient cover for the employee, one spouse, and up to four children, capped at KES 3,000,000 inpatient and KES 300,000 outpatient per family per year.
- **Pension:** Zidipay contributes 10% of gross salary; the employee contributes 5%.
- **Learning budget:** KES 80,000 per employee per year for courses, books, or conferences.
- **Wellness:** Two paid mental-health days per year, separate from sick leave.

## 7. Code of conduct (summary)

All employees must follow the `Anti-Bribery and Code of Conduct` policy in full. Highlights:

- Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination, or retaliation is grounds for termination.
- No gifts above KES 5,000 from any vendor, customer, or counterparty without prior written approval from your manager and Compliance.
- Disclose any conflict of interest (including outside employment) to People Operations within 7 days of becoming aware of it.

## 8. Confidentiality

Employees must protect Zidipay confidential information, customer personal data, and transaction data both during and after employment. See the `Information Security Policy` and the `Data Protection and Privacy Policy` for the rules and the `Onboarding and Offboarding Procedure` for how access is granted and revoked.

## 9. Whistleblowing

Anyone may report suspected misconduct, fraud, or compliance breach in good faith via:

- The anonymous hotline: **+254 711 000 911** (24/7).
- Email: **whistleblow@zidipay.example**, which goes only to the Audit Committee Chair.
- Any member of the People Operations team.

Reports made in good faith will not result in retaliation. False reports made in bad faith are themselves a disciplinary matter.
